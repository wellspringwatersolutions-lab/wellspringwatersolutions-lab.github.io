PFAS, Arsenic & Nitrate Removal for NJ Wells

You can't see, smell, or taste PFAS, arsenic, or nitrate, so testing is the only way to know if they're in your well. If they are, there are treatment options. We'll help you understand them.

PFAS ("forever chemicals")

PFAS are man-made chemicals, sometimes called "forever chemicals," that have been found in some New Jersey wells. Common types include PFOA and PFOS. Treatment options include carbon filtration and reverse osmosis.

  • Can be treated at the kitchen sink or for the whole house
  • The best option depends on your test results
  • Filters need regular changes to keep working

Arsenic

Arsenic occurs naturally in some groundwater in New Jersey. If a test shows arsenic in your well, there are treatment systems designed to reduce it.

  • Options include under-sink reverse osmosis or whole-house treatment
  • Choice depends on your levels and your water chemistry
  • Follow-up testing helps confirm the system is working

Nitrate

Nitrate can get into wells from fertilizer, farm runoff, and septic systems, so it's worth checking in farm areas of South Jersey.

  • Reverse osmosis at the kitchen sink is a common option for drinking water
  • Whole-house options are available when needed
  • Testing first tells us which makes sense

Under-sink reverse osmosis vs. whole-house treatment

An under-sink reverse osmosis (RO) system treats the water you drink and cook with from its own faucet. Whole-house treatment treats all the water coming into your home. Some homes use both.

  • Under-sink RO: compact, treats drinking and cooking water
  • Whole-house: treats every faucet, shower, and appliance
  • We'll walk you through the pros and cons for your home
