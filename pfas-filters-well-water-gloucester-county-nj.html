PFAS Filters and Well Water Treatment in Gloucester County, NJ

Gloucester County has two different well water stories: PFAS near West Deptford, and radium in the rural south. Here’s what the state says about each.

PFAS near West Deptford

  • The state named towns with affected private wells. In its proposed settlement with Solvay over PFAS pollution around West Deptford, New Jersey listed affected private wells in West Deptford, Logan, Deptford, Greenwich, and Swedesboro (NJ Attorney General announcement; NJ DEP Solvay page).
  • PFAS shows up beyond one area, too. Statewide, 11.2% of almost 16,000 wells tested at home sales from December 2021 through 2023 had at least one PFAS over a New Jersey standard (NJ Department of Health PFAS fact sheet).
  • Help may be available. If PFAS in your well is over the state’s relief levels, the NJ Spill Fund may help pay for testing and treatment (same fact sheet).

The state health department lists granular carbon filters and reverse osmosis among the ways to reduce PFAS in drinking water (fact sheet). We install both. More on PFAS treatment.

Radium in the rural townships

Farther south, the issue is radium. The NJ Department of Health lists Franklin and Elk as Gloucester County towns with a high share of wells over the gross alpha limit (NJ Department of Health radium flyer). Across the county, 11% of wells tested from 2002 to 2007 were over that limit (NJ DEP well test results, 2002–2007). PFAS has not been shown to be a problem everywhere, so check the NJ DEP well test maps for your area.

Questions we hear in Gloucester County

Will a regular pitcher filter remove PFAS?

Not reliably. The state health department points to devices certified to NSF/ANSI Standard 53 or 58 for PFAS (fact sheet). We can set up a carbon or reverse osmosis system that fits your test results.

I live in Mullica Hill. Do I have the Solvay PFAS problem?

The state’s list didn’t name Harrison Township. The only way to know about your well is to test it. Start with the state maps or a free in-home water test from us.

Should I test for PFAS if I’m not selling?

The state recommends testing if neighbors have PFAS detections (fact sheet). Use a NJ certified lab for official results.
