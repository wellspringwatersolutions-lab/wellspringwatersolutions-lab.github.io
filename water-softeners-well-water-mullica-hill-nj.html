Water in Mullica Hill

  • Some of Harrison Township is on public water. The township’s water service is run by New Jersey American Water (Harrison Township FAQ).
  • Plenty of homes still have private wells, especially on larger lots and farm roads. State records show 200 Harrison Township wells and 182 South Harrison wells were tested at home sales from 2002 to 2007 (NJ DEP well test results, 2002–2007).
  • Radium is not the problem here. None of those wells in Harrison or South Harrison were over the state limit for gross alpha in that data. So we focus on what local homes do run into: hard water, iron, acidic water, and smells.
  • Want to know about PFAS on your street? The state posts home-sale well results on the NJ DEP well test maps. Look up your area before you guess.

Questions we hear in Mullica Hill

I’m on public water in Mullica Hill. Can you still help?

Yes. Public water can still be hard. A water softener, a carbon filter, or reverse osmosis at the kitchen sink can make a real difference.

How do I know if my home has a well?

If you don’t get a water bill and there’s a pressure tank in your basement or utility room, you likely have a well. We can check during a free in-home water test.
