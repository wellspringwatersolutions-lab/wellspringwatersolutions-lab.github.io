Well Water Treatment in South Jersey

Smells, stains, and scale are common with private wells. Here's what the most common problems usually mean and how we fix them. Every job starts with a free in-home water test.

Rotten egg or sulfur smell

If your water smells like rotten eggs, especially hot water, it's usually sulfur (hydrogen sulfide) in your well. It's one of the most common well water complaints we hear.

  • A strong smell at the tap, shower, or washer
  • Often worse with hot water
  • Treated with the right filter for your water, so the smell is gone at every tap

Orange or red stains: iron in your water

Orange, red, or brown stains in sinks, tubs, toilets, and laundry usually mean iron in your well water. An iron filter removes it before it reaches your fixtures.

  • Rust-colored stains that keep coming back
  • A metallic taste in the water
  • Iron filter sized to how much iron your test shows

Call 856-294-1775 for a free in-home water test

Blue-green stains: acidic water

Blue-green stains under faucets and in tubs usually mean your water is acidic (low pH) and is wearing on copper pipes. An acid neutralizer raises the pH so the water is gentler on your plumbing.

  • Blue-green stains on fixtures and drains
  • Common with private wells in South Jersey
  • Acid neutralizer installed on your main water line

Call 856-294-1775 for a free in-home water test

Hard water: scale and spots

Hard water leaves white scale on faucets and shower doors, spots on dishes, and buildup in your water heater. A water softener removes the minerals that cause it.

  • Less scale on faucets, showerheads, and glass
  • Fewer spots on dishes
  • More about water softeners and salt delivery

Whole house filtration

Whole house filtration treats the water as it comes into your home, so every faucet, shower, and appliance gets better water, not just the kitchen sink.

  • Treats water for the whole home
  • Can be combined with a softener, iron filter, or acid neutralizer
  • Recommended based on what your water test shows
