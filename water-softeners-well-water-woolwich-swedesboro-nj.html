Water in Woolwich and Swedesboro

  • Swedesboro runs its own water system, which serves Swedesboro and parts of Woolwich (Jersey WaterCheck). Other Woolwich homes have private wells. From 2002 to 2007, 358 Woolwich wells were tested at home sales (NJ DEP well test results, 2002–2007).
  • PFAS is the one to watch. The state named Swedesboro among the towns with affected private wells in its proposed PFAS settlement with Solvay (NJ Attorney General announcement).
  • Radium is rare here. Only 1 of those 358 Woolwich wells was over the state limit for gross alpha (NJ DEP well test results, 2002–2007).

If you’re on a well, test for PFAS. The state health department lists carbon filters and reverse osmosis among the ways to reduce it (NJ DOH PFAS fact sheet). If you’re on public water and fighting scale and spots, a water softener is the fix.

Questions we hear in Woolwich & Swedesboro

Does my Woolwich home have PFAS?

Not every home does. The only way to know is a test. Check the NJ DEP well test maps for your area, and use a NJ certified lab for official results.

I’m on public water. Do I need a softener?

Only if your water is hard. If you see white scale on faucets and spots on dishes, a softener helps. Our free in-home water test will tell you.
