City water and wells in Vineland

  • City water covers less than half the city’s land. A state planning report for Vineland puts about 45% of the city’s land in the Vineland public water service area (Vineland state planning report). Many homes on the outskirts use private wells.
  • Radium has turned up in Vineland wells. In older state data, 126 of 362 Vineland wells tested at home sales from 2002 to 2007 (35%) were over the state limit for gross alpha (NJ DEP well test results, 2002–2007). Newer town-by-town results are on the NJ DEP well test maps.
  • Next door is a high-radium area. The NJ Department of Health lists Upper Deerfield, Deerfield, and Hopewell in its high-radium list (NJ Department of Health radium flyer).

The state health department says a water softener or reverse osmosis system can lower radium in well water, and that these systems need upkeep to keep working. In homes it checked in three South Jersey towns, 27% of treatment systems were not bringing radioactivity down to safe levels, likely because of poor maintenance (NJ Department of Health radium flyer). Questions we hear in Vineland

Is Vineland city water hard?

It can be, like a lot of South Jersey water. If you see white scale or spots on dishes, a water softener will help. We can test it for free.

My Vineland home is on a well. What should I test for?

At a minimum: bacteria, nitrate, iron, pH, and gross alpha (radium). Those are on the state home-sale test list for Cumberland County.
